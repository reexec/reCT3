## reCT3 v0.0.16 (English)

Changes since **v0.0.15** (issue #33). **Moves rexbox to v0.0.18, which renders 3D positional audio, voice headroom and a power stereo fold-down.**

Issue: #33 (still open), 3D positional audio and voice headroom (rexbox #40), the commits of this release.

Changes: `third_party/rexbox` from v0.0.17 (`f4a6a48`) to v0.0.18 (`08742ce`): 2D effects drop 6 dB through the default headroom, 3D voices fade with distance, split left and right and gain Doppler, and the mix bins fold to stereo by power (center −3 dB, LFE left out), so the effects stand out less over the music. Documents: design, work order and work log i033, and ARCHITECTURE's submodule line (left at v0.0.14).

Verified: Linux x64 Release with 0 warnings, CTest 7/7 and a 120 s hidden demo without a stop; user listening on Linux: the effects stand out less but still sound off.

Known limits: the effects still sound off (issue #33 stays open), with no HRTF, I3DL2 reverb or original stereo downmix yet; the music repeat during play (issue #29) remains; no regeneration is needed, only a rebuild.
